Reji Japan Travel - Official Information

This page provides the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy for the internal content publishing automation tool operated by Reji Japan Travel.

Terms of Service

1. Overview: Reji Japan Travel Auto Publisher is an internal tool used exclusively by the administrator of Reji Japan Travel to upload short-form travel videos from Google Drive to our official TikTok account via the TikTok Content Posting API.

2. Permitted Use: This tool is strictly for internal operational use and is not offered as a public service to third-party users.

3. Compliance: All published content complies with TikTok's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service.

Privacy Policy

1. Data Collection: This application only accesses authentication tokens and media files belonging to the official Reji Japan Travel account. No personal data from external users or visitors is collected, stored, or shared.

2. Data Usage: OAuth tokens are stored locally and used solely to authenticate API requests for publishing videos to our own TikTok profile.

3. Contact: For any inquiries regarding this application, please contact us via our official TikTok account (@reji_japan_travel).
